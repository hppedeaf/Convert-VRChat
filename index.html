About Our VRChat Credit Converter

What is VRChat Credit Converter?

This free online tool converts VRChat credits (VRC) into real-world currencies using live exchange rates from Open Exchange Rates API. Whether you're a VRChat creator calculating earnings or planning purchases, our converter provides accurate conversions to over 170 currencies.

How VRChat Credits Work

VRChat credits are the virtual currency used within VRChat for purchasing avatar items, world assets, and supporting creators through subscriptions. The standard rate is approximately 120 VRC = $1 USD, but actual values fluctuate with global exchange rates.

Revenue Split Information

When users purchase VRChat credits that go to creators, the revenue is split as follows:

  • 50% goes to content creators
  • 30% goes to platform stores (Steam, Oculus, etc.)
  • 17.1% goes to VRChat
  • 2.9% goes to Tilia (payment processor)
