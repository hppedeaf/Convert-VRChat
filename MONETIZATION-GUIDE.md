## 🎯 Google AdSense Setup (Primary Revenue Source)

### Step 1: Apply for Google AdSense
1. **Visit**: https://www.google.com/adsense/
2. **Click**: "Get Started"
3. **Enter your website**: `https://your-domain.com`
4. **Select country**: Your location
5. **Choose payment currency**: USD recommended

### Step 2: Add Your Site
1. **Add your website** to AdSense
2. **Wait for approval** (typically 7-30 days)
3. **Requirements for approval**:
   - Your site must have **high-quality content** ✅ (Already optimized!)
   - Must have **good user experience** ✅ (Modern design implemented!)
   - Must comply with **AdSense policies** ✅ (Family-friendly content!)
   - Need **sufficient traffic** (aim for 1000+ visitors/month)

### Step 3: Replace Ad Placeholders
Once approved, replace these placeholders in `index.html`:
```html
<!-- Replace this: -->
data-ad-client="ca-pub-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX"

<!-- With your real Publisher ID: -->
data-ad-client="ca-pub-1234567890123456"
```

### Step 4: Ad Slot IDs
Create ad slots in AdSense dashboard and replace:
- `data-ad-slot="1234567890"` (Top banner)
- `data-ad-slot="2345678901"` (Middle content)
- `data-ad-slot="3456789012"` (Bottom banner)
